The formal theorems

Modules are left modules. A Kronecker module (V, W; a, b) is a pair of k-vector spaces of arbitrary dimension with two linear maps V → W, formalized as a functor from WalkingParallelPair to ModuleCat k. The category 𝒟 is the full subcategory where ker a ∩ ker b = 0. Dimensions are cardinals, given by Module.rank. All types live in one universe.

A representation embedding is a k-linear functor that preserves short exact sequences and indecomposability and reflects isomorphism classes. A is representation-finite if finitely many modules represent all finitely generated indecomposable modules.
