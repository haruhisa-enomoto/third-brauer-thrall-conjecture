The proof in four parts

The paper defines the three functors behind Bongartz's embedding directly on Kronecker modules of arbitrary dimension, recovers Kronecker morphisms from morphisms between their images by retractions with nilpotent kernels, and cancels the images of the simple projective summands. The library then proves the two inputs that the paper cites.

Reduction

The steps of the proof

Ray categories and standardness

Over a representation-finite quotient, the standard form is representation-finite (Bretscher–Gabriel). A minimal algebra has a twisted multiplicative basis over its ray category, and mild contours give standardness.
