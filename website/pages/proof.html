The steps of the proof

Ray categories and standardness

Over a representation-finite quotient, the standard form is representation-finite (Bretscher–Gabriel). A minimal algebra has a twisted multiplicative basis over its ray category, and mild contours give standardness.

Critical pieces

A critical convex piece of the universal cover carries a point and a brick with two extensions. The proof uses no tilting theory.
